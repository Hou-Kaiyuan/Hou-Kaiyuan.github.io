I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in [Electrical Engineering at Columbia University](https://www.ee.columbia.edu/), advised by [Prof. Xiaofan (Fred) Jiang](http://fredjiang.com/) in the [Intelligent and Connected Systems Lab](http://icsl.ee.columbia.edu/) (ICSL). I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/ecee/), *summa cum laude* with highest honors, with minors in Physics, Economics, and Computer Science.

My research builds **intelligent systems that connect foundation models to the physical world**. I work across three threads: (1) *agentic and embodied AI* — agents that perceive, decide, and act through real sensing and actuation; (2) *multimodal foundation models* — how models reason over non-traditional inputs such as aerial imagery, egocentric video, IMU, and IoT sensor streams, and how to make that reasoning reliable and efficient; and (3) *human-centered sensing* — deployable systems for health and the built environment.

**I expect to graduate in December 2026 and am on the job market for full-time roles starting January 2027.** Feel free to reach out at [kh3119@columbia.edu](mailto:kh3119@columbia.edu).
